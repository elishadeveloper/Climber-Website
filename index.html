Welcome to Mount Climbers

The Mount Climbers is a national club whose objectives are to encourage mountaineering and rock-climbing, and to promote the general interests of mountaineers and the mountain environment. Applications for membership are welcomed from committed and experienced climbers from all sectors of the community.



Ice Mountain Climbing & Safety Guide

Ice mountain climbing is a challenging adventure that requires proper training, experience, and preparation. Climbers should always check weather and avalanche conditions before starting and use essential equipment such as crampons, an ice axe, helmet, harness, and appropriate clothing. Always climb with an experienced partner or guide, maintain secure footing, and avoid unstable ice or steep routes beyond your skill level. Carry navigation tools, emergency supplies, and sufficient food and water, and be prepared to turn back if conditions become dangerous.
