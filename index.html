Choosing the Right Tent

A camping tent provides protection from changing weather conditions. Choose a strong, waterproof tent suitable for the expected temperature and wind.

Finding a Safe Campsite

Always select a flat and stable location for your campsite. Avoid steep slopes, unstable rocks, dry riverbeds and avalanche-prone areas.

Preparing for Camping

Before starting your journey, pack essential equipment such as warm clothing, sleeping bags, drinking water, food, a flashlight and a first aid kit.

Mountain Camping Safety Tips

Stay Safe in the Mountains

Mountain weather can change unexpectedly. Proper planning and preparation can help you avoid dangerous situations during camping.

  • Always check the weather forecast.
  • Carry enough food and clean drinking water.
  • Choose a safe place to set up your tent.
  • Keep emergency supplies with you.
  • Never leave your campsite dirty.
  • Inform someone about your travel plans.
  • Never use camping stoves inside closed tents.
